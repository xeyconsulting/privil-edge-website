Who we are

This website is operated by Privil Edge Solutions, Kochi, Kerala, India. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Privil Edge Solutions.

What we collect

When you submit the callback form, we collect:

  • your name, mobile number and city (optional);
  • the loan type you select;
  • the loan amount, tenure, interest rate and estimated EMI shown in the calculator at the time you submit;
  • if you used the eligibility check, your selected income range, employment type and the indicative result.

The calculator and eligibility check run in your browser. Nothing is sent to us unless you submit the form.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry and discuss financing options.

  • To keep records of enquiries and improve our service.

We do not sell your personal information.

Form submissions are delivered to us by Web3Forms, a form-handling service, which passes the details to our email. We may share your details with lending partners only to process your enquiry, and only with your consent.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools. If this changes, we will update this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond to you and meet our legal and business record-keeping requirements.
