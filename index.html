FAQ

Questions, answered.

Quick answers before you apply.

What documents do I usually need?

Typically a photo ID, address proof, recent income proof or bank statements and, for a loan against car, the vehicle's registration certificate and insurance. Exact requirements depend on the lender and your profile, and our team confirms the list when they call you back.

What is the starting interest rate?

Rates start from 7.5% p.a. The rate you receive depends on your profile, the vehicle and the lender. Use the EMI calculator above to see how different rates change your monthly payment.

What is a loan against car?

It lets you raise funds against a car you already own while you continue to use it. The amount depends on the vehicle's value and condition and on your repayment capacity.

How long does approval take?

Timelines depend on the lender and how quickly your documents are verified. Our team will give you a realistic estimate when they call you back.

Does the eligibility check affect my credit score?

No. The eligibility check on this page is only an indicative estimate calculated in your browser. It does not involve a credit bureau check.

Are there processing fees or other charges?

Processing and other charges vary by lender and loan type. Our team explains the applicable charges before you proceed, so you can compare with confidence.

Can I repay the loan early?

Many lenders allow part-payment or foreclosure, sometimes with charges. The terms depend on the lender, and we will share them before you decide.

How do I get started?

Use the calculator, run the eligibility check, then request a callback. You can also message us on WhatsApp or call +91 97785 16374.
